LEVEL 5 LANDSLIDE WARNING ISSUED FOR JAPAN’S OSHIMA TOWN

Japan’s Meteorological Agency issued a Level 5 Special Landslide Warning for Oshima Town in Tokyo’s northern Izu Islands on September 21, warning that life-threatening conditions may already exist in landslide-risk areas.

Japan’s Meteorological Agency (JMA) issued a Level 5 Special Landslide Warning for Oshima Town in Tokyo’s Izu Islands on September 21, placing areas at risk of landslides under Japan’s highest level of emergency warning.

JMA said that in designated landslide-danger areas, the possibility is extremely high that a disaster may already have occurred. The agency warned that lives may be in immediate danger and urged people to secure their safety without delay.

JMA also cautioned that an officially designated evacuation location may itself be dangerous in some circumstances. Where necessary, residents should move to a building farther away from cliffs and ravines or otherwise take immediate action to protect themselves.

The agency urged residents to follow evacuation information issued by local municipalities and to monitor detailed regional weather information. JMA also directed people to use its Kikikuru hazard-information system to check the level of disaster risk at their current location.

JMA emphasized that people should not wait for a Level 5 announcement before taking protective action when local evacuation information has already been issued.
