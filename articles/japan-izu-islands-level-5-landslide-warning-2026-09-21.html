Japan Issues Level 5 Landslide Warning for Oshima Town in Northern Izu Islands

The Japan Meteorological Agency issued a Level 5 special landslide warning for Oshima Town in Tokyo's northern Izu Islands on September 21, 2026, warning of an extremely dangerous situation and imminent danger to life.

Article body

The Japan Meteorological Agency said on September 21, 2026, that it had issued a Level 5 special landslide warning for Oshima Town in Tokyo's northern Izu Islands.

JMA said that, particularly in areas designated as landslide-danger zones, the possibility was extremely high that some disaster may already have occurred. The agency described the situation as corresponding to Warning Level 5, with imminent danger to life and a need to secure safety immediately.

JMA advised that if reaching a designated evacuation location could itself be dangerous, people should move to a building farther away from cliffs or ravines or otherwise secure their safety.

The agency also said maximum caution was necessary even in places where people would not normally expect a disaster. JMA noted that Level 5 special warnings could potentially be issued to other municipalities and urged people not to wait for a special warning before taking protective action where local municipalities had already issued evacuation information.

JMA directed people to check detailed regional information from local meteorological offices and to use its Kikikuru hazard information to check the disaster risk at their current location.
